About Me

I am currently an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Chuanxia Zheng. I obtained my B.Eng. in Internet of Things Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, in a joint programme with Queen Mary University of London. During my undergraduate study, I worked with Prof. Xiaolong Zheng on UWB-based close-range sensing.

My research interests span machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent sensing systems. I am particularly interested in using learning-based methods to understand physical signals and build reliable perception systems for real-world IoT scenarios.
