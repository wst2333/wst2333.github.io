Biography

I am currently a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence supervised by Prof. Chuanxia Zheng at Physical Vision Group, College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

Before coming to NTU, I obtained my B.Eng. in Internet of Things Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) and Queen Mary University of London (QMUL) through their joint programme. During my undergraduate study, I was fortunate to work with Prof. Xiaolong Zheng on UWB-based close-range sensing.

My research interests span machine learning, computer vision, and intelligent sensing systems. I am particularly interested in learning-based methods for understanding physical signals and building reliable perception systems for real-world IoT scenarios.
